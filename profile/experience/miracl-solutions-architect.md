Whilst in the role of Solutions Architect at MIRACL, my responsibilities involved working with sales and potential clients to assist in the implementation of the company's cryptographic solutions. This included working closely with Okta to assist in the implementation of M-Pin within the Okta SSO solution via OAuth 2 and a custom identity server.

I also worked on R&D for the use of M-Pin in a number of areas, such as Windows authentication.

This was also the first time I had exposure to .NET Core, which was in early beta at the time, when I attempted to port the M-Pin backend services with the intention of running them on Linux. Unfortunately this project hit a wall because SSL had not been implemented at that stage.
